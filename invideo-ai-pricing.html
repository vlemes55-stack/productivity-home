What is InVideo AI?

InVideo AI is an AI-powered video creation platform that automates the process of turning text prompts into fully edited videos. Unlike traditional video editors, it uses generative AI to script, design, and render videos in minutes, making it ideal for content creators, marketers, and businesses.

Key capabilities include:

  • AI-generated scripts from text prompts

  • Auto-selection of stock media (images, videos, music)

  • Smart editing (cuts, transitions, captions)

  • Voiceover generation with AI voices

  • Multi-language support (50+ languages)

Why people choose InVideo AI:

  • Saves time: No need to manually edit or source clips

  • Reduces costs: Eliminates hiring editors or buying expensive software

  • Scalable: Generate multiple videos quickly for campaigns

🔹 Note: InVideo AI is part of the InVideo ecosystem, which also includes the classic InVideo editor for manual editing.

How Does InVideo AI Pricing Work?

InVideo AI operates on a credit-based system, where each AI task (e.g., generating a script, selecting media, or rendering a video) consumes credits. The pricing model is structured around monthly subscriptions with tiered access to AI credits, storage, and features.

Core pricing rules:

  • Credits reset monthly (unused credits don’t roll over)

  • Free plan includes a limited number of credits

  • Paid plans unlock more credits, higher storage, and advanced features

  • Enterprise plans offer custom credit packages and dedicated support

When do you need paid plans?

  • If you generate more than 10 videos/month

  • If you require high-quality exports without watermarks

  • If you need priority support or bulk media access

Frequently Asked Questions About InVideo AI Pricing


What happens if I run out of AI credits?


If you exhaust your monthly credits, you can either upgrade your plan or purchase additional credits. Your videos will remain in your dashboard, but you won’t be able to generate new ones until credits reset (or you add more).

Can I use InVideo AI for commercial projects?


Yes! Both the Plus and Max plans allow commercial use. The free plan includes a watermark on exports, which may not be suitable for branding.

How does the free trial work?


InVideo AI doesn’t offer a traditional free trial, but the free plan serves as a no-risk way to test its features. You can try it here.

Is there a discount for students or nonprofits?


InVideo occasionally offers educational discounts for students and nonprofits. Check their pricing page or contact support for eligibility.

Can I downgrade or upgrade my plan anytime?


Yes! You can switch plans at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and unused credits from your current plan may carry over (depending on the new plan).

What’s the difference between AI credits and video exports?

  • AI credits are used for generating scripts, selecting media, and rendering videos.

  • Video exports refer to the final rendered video file (e.g., 720p, 1080p, 4K).

  • Some plans limit exports by resolution (e.g., free plan only allows 720p).

Do credits roll over if I don’t use them?

No. AI credits reset monthly and do not roll over to the next month. However, unused paid plans (e.g., if you downgrade) may provide a prorated refund for the remaining period.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

InVideo AI offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for paid plans. If you’re not satisfied, you can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Can teams use the same account?

The Plus plan supports 1 user, but teams can share the account with caution. The Max plan supports up to 5 users, and Enterprise plans offer unlimited users with dedicated support.

What payment methods does InVideo AI accept?

InVideo AI accepts credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.
