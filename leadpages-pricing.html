How to Cancel Your Leadpages Subscription Without Penalties

Canceling your Leadpages subscription is straightforward, but follow these steps to avoid issues:

  1. Log in to your Leadpages account.

  2. Go to "Billing" in the dashboard.

  3. Click "Cancel Subscription" under your plan.

  4. Choose a reason (optional feedback).

  5. Confirm cancellation—your access continues until the end of the billing cycle.

✅ Important Notes:

  • No refunds for partial months (prorated billing).

  • Downgrading is possible (e.g., Pro → Standard).

  • Reactivating within 30 days keeps your pages intact.

🚨 Common Mistake: Forgetting to cancel before the 14-day trial ends can lead to automatic charges. Set a calendar reminder!

Frequently Asked Questions About Leadpages Pricing


Is there a Leadpages free plan?


No, Leadpages does not offer a free plan. However, the 14-day free trial gives you full access to all features with no credit card required. This is the best way to test the tool risk-free.

Can I pay monthly or must I commit to a year?


Leadpages offers both monthly and annual billing. Annual plans save up to 20% and lock in the current rate for 12 months. Monthly billing is more flexible but costs more long-term.

Do Leadpages plans include a domain?


Yes! All plans support custom domains (e.g., yourdomain.com). The Standard plan includes one domain, while Pro and Advanced allow multiple.

What happens after the 14-day trial ends?


If you don’t upgrade, your access to the account and pages is disabled. You can reactivate anytime within 30 days without losing your work.

Are there discounts for nonprofits or students?


Yes! Leadpages offers special discounts for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact their support team for eligibility details.

Can I downgrade my plan later?


Absolutely. You can downgrade from Pro/Advanced to Standard anytime, but you won’t get a refund for the difference. Upgrading is also possible at any time.

Does Leadpages charge for extra leads or traffic?


No! All plans include unlimited leads and traffic. You won’t face overage fees, no matter how many visitors your pages receive.

Is Leadpages worth the price compared to alternatives?


For small businesses and marketers, Leadpages is a strong choice due to its ease of use and template library. However, if you need deep automation or CRM features, tools like ClickFunnels or HubSpot may be better. Compare based on your specific needs.
