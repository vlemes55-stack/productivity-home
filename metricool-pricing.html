How to Set Up Metricool & Get Started (Step-by-Step)

Ready to try Metricool? Here’s a simple step-by-step guide to get started in under 10 minutes.

🚀 Step 1: Sign Up & Choose a Plan

  1. Go to Metricool’s pricing page.

  2. Select your plan (Free, Starter, Advanced, or Business).

  3. Enter your email and create a password.

🔗 Step 2: Connect Your Social Accounts

  1. Click "Add Social Account" in the dashboard.

  2. Select the platform (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.).

  3. Authorize Metricool to access your account (follow the prompts).

✍️ Step 3: Schedule Your First Post

  1. Click "Create Post" in the top-right corner.

  2. Select the social accounts you want to post to.

  3. Write your caption or use Metricool’s AI tool to generate one.

  4. Upload your image/video and set the publish date/time.

  5. Click "Schedule" to finalize.

📊 Step 4: Monitor Performance

  1. Go to the "Analytics" tab in your dashboard.

  2. Check metrics like engagement rate, follower growth, and top-performing posts.

  3. Use insights to adjust your strategy.

🤝 Step 5: Invite Team Members (Paid Plans Only)

  1. Go to "Settings" > "Team."

  2. Enter the team member’s email address.

  3. Set permissions (e.g., admin, editor, viewer).

  4. Click "Invite."

⚡ Time-Saving Tips:

  • Use Metricool’s bulk uploader to schedule multiple posts at once (Advanced/Business plans).

  • Enable AI caption suggestions to speed up content creation.

  • Set up automated reports to save time on analytics.

Metricool Pricing FAQ: Common Questions Answered


❓ Does Metricool offer a free plan?


Yes! Metricool’s Free plan includes 1 social account, 10 scheduled posts/month, and basic analytics. It’s a great way to test the platform before upgrading.

❓ Can I upgrade my plan later?


Absolutely. You can switch plans at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and you’ll be billed pro-rated for the remainder of your current billing cycle.

❓ Is there a discount for annual billing?


Yes! Metricool offers up to 20% off when you choose annual billing. For example, the Advanced plan drops from $20/month to $16/month when paid annually.

❓ Does Metricool include Instagram scheduling?


Yes. Metricool supports scheduling for Instagram (posts, Reels, Stories), Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter (X), YouTube, and TikTok (though TikTok integration is less robust than native tools).

❓ Can I use Metricool for multiple brands?


Yes, but it depends on your plan:

  • Starter: Up to 15 brands/accounts.

  • Advanced: Up to 30 brands/accounts.

  • Business: Unlimited brands/accounts.

❓ Does Metricool have an API?

Yes, but only on the Business plan ($300/month). API access allows you to integrate Metricool with other tools like CRM systems or custom dashboards.

❓ Is Metricool better than Hootsuite?

Metricool wins in affordability and simplicity, but Hootsuite offers more advanced features (e.g., social listening, more integrations). Choose Metricool if you prioritize cost and ease of use; choose Hootsuite if you need enterprise-level tools.

❓ Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Metricool allows canceling at any time with no penalties. You’ll lose access at the end of your current billing cycle.

❓ Does Metricool offer customer support?

Yes. All paid plans include priority support (email/chat). Business plan users get dedicated account managers for large teams.

❓ Is Metricool worth the price?

For small businesses and solopreneurs, Metricool’s Advanced plan ($20/month) is a great value. For agencies or large teams, the Business plan ($300/month) justifies its cost with API access and unlimited accounts.
