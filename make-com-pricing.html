What is Make.com Pricing? Core Plans & Cost Structure

Make.com operates on a usage-based pricing model, where costs scale with the number of operations performed each month. An operation refers to any action the platform executes, such as transferring data between apps, sending notifications, or processing files. This model ensures teams only pay for what they use, making it ideal for startups and growing businesses.

Key Pricing Components

  1. Operations: The primary cost driver. Each task (e.g., API call, data transformation) counts as an operation.

  2. Users: Some plans include multiple team members; others charge per user.

  3. Data Volume: Larger data transfers may incur additional fees.

  4. Custom Integrations: Complex workflows requiring custom code may need enterprise pricing.

Why this matters: Unlike flat-rate tools, Make.com’s pricing adapts to your team’s actual workload. For example, a marketing team running 5,000 operations/month won’t overpay for unused capacity.

Make.com Pricing FAQs


Is Make.com’s free plan really free?


Yes, Make.com’s free plan includes 1,000 operations/month, 100 MB storage, and access to basic features. However, it’s limited to one user and lacks advanced tools like priority support or custom integrations. For teams needing more, paid plans start at $9/month.

Can I pay annually for Make.com?


Yes, Make.com offers annual billing for all paid plans, which includes a 10–20% discount compared to monthly payments. For example, the Core plan costs $9/month or $99/year—saving $18 over 12 months.

Does Make.com charge per user?


No, only the Teams plan charges per user ($29/user/month). All other plans (Core, Pro, Enterprise) are priced per account, regardless of the number of collaborators. This makes Make.com cost-effective for small teams sharing a single account.

What happens if I exceed my operation limit?


If you exceed your monthly operations limit, Make.com temporarily disables your account until the next billing cycle. You’ll need to upgrade your plan or wait for the reset to resume operations. Pro tip: Set up usage alerts in your dashboard to avoid disruptions.

Is Make.com cheaper than Zapier for automation?


Yes, for most use cases. Make.com’s Core plan ($9/month) includes 10,000 operations, while Zapier’s Starter plan ($19.99/month) only offers 750 operations. For teams handling high-volume workflows, Make.com can save 50–80% compared to Zapier.

Can I downgrade my Make.com plan anytime?


Yes, you can downgrade or upgrade your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle, and you’ll receive a prorated refund if you’ve overpaid. This flexibility ensures you only pay for what you need.

Does Make.com offer discounts for nonprofits or students?


Yes, Make.com provides discounted plans for nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact their sales team to learn about eligibility and pricing. Common discounts range from 10–30% off standard plans.

How does Make.com handle data privacy and security?


Make.com is GDPR, SOC 2, and HIPAA compliant, with encryption for data at rest and in transit. Enterprise plans include dedicated security audits and SLA-backed uptime guarantees. For highly sensitive data, custom plans can be tailored with additional security measures.

Can I use Make.com for sales or marketing automations?


Absolutely. Make.com excels at sales and marketing automations, including CRM updates, email triggers, lead nurturing, and social media scheduling. The platform’s visual builder makes it easy to design complex workflows without coding.

What customer support options does Make.com offer?

  • Free Plan: Community forum and email support.

  • Core/Pro Plans: Email support with 24-hour response time.

  • Teams/Enterprise Plans: Priority email and chat support, with dedicated account managers for Enterprise users.
