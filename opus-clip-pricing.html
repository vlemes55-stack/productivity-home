❓ What Is Opus Clip Pricing and How Does It Work?

Opus Clip pricing is tiered based on video processing credits, clip output limits, and team access. Each plan includes a set number of AI-generated clips per month, with credits consumed for features like auto-captioning, scene detection, and social media optimization.

Key factors affecting cost:

  • Clip output limits (e.g., Free: 10 clips/month, Pro: 200 clips/month).

  • Video length restrictions (e.g., Free: 10 minutes, Pro: 60 minutes).

  • AI features (e.g., custom captions, auto-clipping, brand templates).

  • Team collaboration (available in Team and Enterprise plans).

  • Export options (watermark-free exports, multiple format support).

Opus Clip operates on a credit system, where each minute of video processed consumes credits. The more advanced the AI features (e.g., multi-angle clipping, auto-captions), the more credits are used per clip.

> 💡 Pro Tip: Annual billing reduces costs by up to 20%, making it ideal for long-term users. Monthly plans are better for testing the platform before committing.

🤔 Frequently Asked Questions About Opus Clip Pricing


1. Does Opus Clip offer a free trial for paid plans?


No, Opus Clip does not offer a free trial for its paid plans. However, the Free plan provides full access to basic AI clipping features with limitations. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, but there’s no traditional trial period.

2. Can I get a discount for annual billing?


Yes! Annual billing saves up to 20% compared to monthly payments. For example:

  • Pro plan: $39/month → $31.20/month (annual).

  • Team plan: $99/month → $79.20/month (annual).

3. What happens if I run out of credits?

If you hit your monthly clip limit, Opus Clip will stop processing new clips until the next billing cycle or until you refill credits (available in higher-tier plans). Upgrading to a higher plan increases your credit limit.

4. Is Opus Clip worth the cost for small businesses?

Yes, if:

  • You repurpose long-form content (e.g., webinars, tutorials) into social clips.

  • You need team collaboration (Team plan).

  • You want watermark-free exports (Starter or higher).

  • You process 10+ videos per month.

No, if:

  • You only need manual clipping (CapCut is free).

  • Your videos are short (under 10 minutes).

  • You don’t need AI automation.
