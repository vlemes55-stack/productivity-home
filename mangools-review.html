Mangools is an all-in-one SEO toolkit designed for keyword research, rank tracking, backlink analysis, and SERP analysis. Launched in 2014, it’s now used by over 1.5 million users, including marketers, bloggers, and small agencies.   • KWFinder: Finds low-competition keywords with difficulty scores.

  • SERPWatcher: Tracks keyword rankings across search engines.

  • LinkMiner: Checks backlinks and broken links in seconds.

  • SiteProfiler: Evaluates website authority and backlink profiles.

The platform is known for its clean interface, fast speeds, and competitive pricing. It’s ideal for SEOs who need reliable data without overwhelming complexity.

Mangools Tools Breakdown: KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher & More

1. KWFinder: The Best Keyword Research Tool for Beginners?

KWFinder is Mangools’ flagship tool for finding profitable keywords. It’s praised for its simple UI and accurate difficulty scores, making it a favorite for bloggers and small businesses.

⚡ Key Features:

  • Keyword suggestions: Generates long-tail and related keywords.

  • Keyword difficulty: Scores keywords from 0 (easy) to 100 (hard).

  • SERP analysis: Shows top-ranking pages and their metrics.

  • Local keyword research: Filters results by country, city, or language.

  • CPC & search volume data: Helps estimate traffic potential.

🔍 Example: Searching for "best running shoes" in the U.S. reveals keywords like "best running shoes for flat feet" (difficulty: 34) and "best trail running shoes" (difficulty: 42).

Pros:
✅ Intuitive and beginner-friendly.
✅ Accurate difficulty scores (tested against Ahrefs and SEMrush).
✅ Fast and lightweight—no lag like competitors.

Cons:
❌ Limited historical data in basic plans.
❌ No AI-powered keyword clustering (unlike newer tools).

2. SERPChecker: Analyze Search Results Like a Pro

SERPChecker lets you dissect Google’s search results page by page. It’s useful for competitive analysis and finding ranking opportunities.

⚡ Key Features:

  • SERP analysis: Shows top 10 results with metrics (DA, backlinks, traffic estimates).

  • Local & global search: Filters results by location.

  • SERP features: Highlights featured snippets, PAA boxes, and ads.

  • Competitor gap analysis: Compares your site to top competitors.

🔍 Example: Analyzing "best budget laptops" shows which pages rank, their backlink counts, and potential content gaps.

Pros:
✅ Clean, distraction-free interface.
✅ Helps identify low-competition topics quickly.

Cons:
❌ No AI-powered insights (e.g., content optimization suggestions).
❌ Limited to 10 results per search in basic plans.

3. SERPWatcher: Rank Tracking Made Simple

SERPWatcher tracks your keyword rankings across search engines. It’s straightforward and ideal for monitoring SEO progress.

⚡ Key Features:

  • Daily rank updates: Tracks changes in real-time.

  • Mobile vs. desktop tracking: Separates rankings by device.

  • Competitor comparison: Shows how you stack up against rivals.

  • Alerts: Notifies you of ranking drops or spikes.

🔍 Example: If you track "best running shoes," SERPWatcher notifies you if your site drops from position 8 to 12.

Pros:
✅ No complex setup required.
✅ Affordable compared to Ahrefs or SEMrush.

Cons:
❌ No advanced filtering (e.g., by search intent).
❌ Limited historical data in lower-tier plans.

4. LinkMiner: Backlink Analysis in Seconds

LinkMiner helps you analyze backlinks and find broken links. It’s a lightweight alternative to Ahrefs’ Site Explorer or Majestic.

⚡ Key Features:

  • Backlink checker: Shows referring domains, anchor texts, and authority scores.

  • Broken link finder: Identifies 404 errors on any website.

  • Competitor backlink analysis: Spies on rivals’ link profiles.

  • Filtering options: Sort by DA, traffic, or link type.

🔍 Example: Entering a competitor’s URL reveals their top backlinks—useful for link-building strategies.

Pros:
✅ Fast and easy to use.
✅ Great for quick backlink audits.

Cons:
❌ No link-building outreach tools (unlike Ahrefs).
❌ Limited to 10,000 backlinks/month in basic plans.

5. SiteProfiler: Measure Website Authority & Backlinks

SiteProfiler evaluates a website’s strength based on backlinks and traffic. It’s useful for vetting link prospects or analyzing competitors.

⚡ Key Features:

  • Domain Authority (DA) score: Estimates site credibility.

  • Backlink profile: Shows top referring domains and anchor texts.

  • Traffic estimates: Predicts monthly organic traffic.

  • Competitor comparison: Compares multiple sites side by side.

🔍 Example: Comparing two blogs in the fitness niche shows which has stronger backlinks and higher traffic potential.

Pros:
✅ Quick authority checks.
✅ Helps identify high-quality link sources.

Cons:
❌ No deep link analysis (unlike Ahrefs).
❌ Data may lag behind competitors like Moz.
