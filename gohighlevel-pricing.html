What is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel is an all-in-one CRM, marketing automation, and white-label platform designed for agencies, freelancers, and SaaS businesses. Unlike competitors, it offers unlimited sub accounts, meaning you can manage multiple clients under one account without extra fees.

  • White-label branding for agencies

  • Built-in CRM with pipeline management

  • Automation tools (SMS, email, workflows)

  • API access for custom integrations

Hidden Fees & Surprise Costs in GoHighLevel

Some costs aren’t obvious in GoHighLevel’s pricing page. Here’s what to watch for:

1. SMS & Email Credits

  • Starter Plan: 1,000 credits/month (included)

  • Agency Plan: 5,000 credits/month (included)

  • SaaS Pro Plan: 10,000 credits/month (included)

  • Extra credits: $0.008 per SMS, $0.001 per email

Example: Sending 10,000 SMS messages/month = $80 extra on the Starter plan.

2. API Calls Beyond Limit

  • Starter: 5,000 calls/month (included)

  • Agency: 10,000 calls/month (included)

  • SaaS Pro: 25,000 calls/month (included)

  • Extra calls: $0.01 per 1,000 calls

Example: 50,000 API calls on Agency plan = $25 extra.

3. White-Label Upgrades

  • Full white-label: $297 one-time fee (removes GoHighLevel branding)

4. Additional Domains

  • Custom domains: Included (1 per plan)

  • Extra domains: $20/month each

5. Team Members

  • Starter Plan: 1 user only

  • Agency Plan: Up to 5 users

  • SaaS Pro Plan: Unlimited users

  • Extra users: $97/month per additional user

🚨 Pro Tip: Use the SMS credit calculator before committing to a plan. If you send lots of SMS, the Agency or SaaS Pro plan may save you money long-term.

How to Cancel GoHighLevel & Refund Policy

Canceling GoHighLevel is straightforward, but refunds are not guaranteed:

  1. Log in to your GoHighLevel account

  2. Go to Settings > Billing

  3. Click "Cancel Subscription"

  4. Select a reason (optional)

  5. Confirm cancellation

Refund Policy:

  • No refunds for partial months

  • Pro-rated refunds only if canceled within 3 days of billing

  • Annual plans are non-refundable

🚨 Warning: If you cancel, you’ll lose access to all data after the billing cycle ends. Export your contacts and campaigns before canceling.
