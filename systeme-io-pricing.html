How to Set Up a Custom Domain in Systeme.io

Custom domains are only available on the Webinar ($47) and Unlimited ($97) plans. Here’s how to set one up:

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Purchase a domain (e.g., via Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Cloudflare).

  2. In Systeme.io: Go to Settings > Domains > Add Domain.

  3. Verify ownership by adding a DNS record (TXT or CNAME).

  4. Set up SSL (automatic for most domains).

  5. Update your funnel/email domains to use your custom domain.

> ⚠️ Pro Tip: Use Cloudflare for free SSL and faster DNS propagation. Total setup takes under 10 minutes.

Frequent Questions About Systeme.io Pricing


1. Does Systeme.io charge per contact?

No! All plans include unlimited contacts—unlike competitors like Mailchimp or Kajabi, which charge extra after a certain limit. This makes Systeme.io highly scalable for growing businesses.

2. What’s the difference between the Startup and Webinar plans?

The Startup plan ($27) includes unlimited funnels and email campaigns but no custom domains or webinar automation. The Webinar plan ($47) adds custom domains, webinar automation, and A/B testing—ideal for coaches and course creators.

3. Can I downgrade my plan later?

Yes! Systeme.io allows downgrades at any time without losing data. Your billing cycle adjusts automatically, and you won’t lose access to features mid-cycle.

4. Are there transaction fees on the Free plan?

Yes—the Free plan has a 3% transaction fee on sales. All paid plans (Startup and up) have 0% transaction fees, making them ideal for e-commerce or affiliate marketing.

5. Does Systeme.io offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes! All paid plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. No risk—just cancel if it doesn’t meet your needs.

6. Can I use my own domain with the Free plan?

No—custom domains are only available on paid plans (Webinar and Unlimited). If you need a custom domain, upgrade to one of these plans.

8. Is Systeme.io suitable for agencies?

Absolutely! The Unlimited plan ($97/month) is designed for agencies, offering:

9. What payment methods does Systeme.io accept?

Systeme.io accepts credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers (for annual plans). All payments are processed securely via Stripe.
